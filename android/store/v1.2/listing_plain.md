# Kart Racing - Google Play listing (plain text, no emojis or special characters)

## App name

Kart Racing: Car Race Offline

## Short description

Fun kart racing for kids and family. Drift, boost and beat 7 rivals offline.

## Full description

Kart Racing is a fun 3D go kart racing game you can play offline, anywhere. No wifi, no internet, just jump in and race.

Pick your racer, line up against 7 computer rivals and race your kart to the finish line. Drift around corners, grab power-ups, hit nitro and boost past every car on the track. It is one of the easiest racing games to pick up, with harder levels when you are ready for a real challenge.

CAR RACING YOUR WAY
- Kart Race: finish first in a classic car race full of power-ups
- Time Trial: just you, your kart and the clock
- Follow the Leader: stay close to the leader but do not overtake
- Battle Arena: hit your rivals and be the last kart driving

DRIFT AND BOOST
Hold drift through the turns and let go for a speed boost. Collect nitro bottles and fire them on the straights. Master drifting and you will win every race.

3D RACING TRACKS
Race on snowy mountain roads, sunny castle lakes, a desert, a zen garden and even a giant classroom. Every racing track has shortcuts to find.

POWER-UPS
Drive through gift boxes for bowling balls, cakes, plungers and more surprises, then blast the cars in front of you.

STORY MODE
The evil Nolok has captured Gnu. Win challenges, earn cups and unlock new karts and race tracks in a full story adventure.

EASY CONTROLS
- Steer with the on-screen wheel or tilt your phone
- Auto accelerate, so you only need to steer and drift
- Big buttons and clean menus for kids and adults
- Four difficulty levels, from Novice to Legend
- Plays in your phone's language

OFFLINE GAME, NO WIFI NEEDED
Kart Racing works fully offline. Perfect for car trips, flights, school breaks and anywhere without internet.

If you love kart games, car games and fast racing games, start your engine and race to the finish.

Kart Racing is based on SuperTuxKart, the open-source kart racer, released under the GNU GPL v3.
