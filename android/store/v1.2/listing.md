## Full description (4000 characters max)

Ready, set, GO! 🏁 Kart Race is a fast, colourful kart racing game you can play anywhere — no internet needed.

Line up against 7 computer rivals, drift through tight corners, grab power-ups and boost past everyone to the finish line. Easy to pick up for kids, with hard difficulty levels that keep experienced racers coming back.

🏎️ RACE YOUR WAY
• Normal Race — all power-ups allowed, finish first to win
• Time Trial — no power-ups, just you and the clock
• Follow the Leader — stay close to the leader but don't overtake
• Battle — hit your rivals and be the last kart driving

🌍 STUNNING TRACKS
Race through snowy mountain resorts, sunny castle lakes, peaceful zen gardens, desert sands and even a giant classroom. Every track has shortcuts to discover.

🎁 POWER-UPS AND NITRO
Drive through gift boxes for bowling balls, cakes, plungers and more. Collect nitro and fire it at the right moment for a huge burst of speed.

📖 STORY MODE
The evil Nolok has captured Gnu! Explore the story world, complete challenges, win cups and unlock new tracks and karts on the way to the final race.

🐧 PICK YOUR RACER
Choose from a crew of fun characters. Light karts accelerate fast, heavy karts hit hard, so find the one that suits your style.

📱 MADE FOR PHONES
• Simple touch controls with a steering wheel, or tilt your phone to steer
• Auto-accelerate, so you can focus on steering and drifting
• Clean modern menus with big buttons
• Four difficulty levels, from Novice to Legend
• Plays in your phone's language

✈️ PLAY OFFLINE
No Wi-Fi? No problem. Kart Race works fully offline — perfect for trips, flights and waiting rooms.

Kart Race is based on SuperTuxKart, the free and open-source kart racer, and is released under the GNU GPL v3.

Tap install and start your engines! 🏆
