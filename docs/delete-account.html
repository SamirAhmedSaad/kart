Delete your data

Car Race Offline – by Samir Hayan

You don't need an account to play Car Race Offline, and the game has no online accounts. Your progress is saved only on your phone.

Delete game data on your phone

Open Android Settings → Apps → Car Race Offline → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. This deletes your settings, progress, high scores and downloaded tracks. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

What is kept

Samir Hayan keeps no copy of your data. Questions: samir.hayan@gmail.com
