Privacy Policy for Car Race Offline

Effective date: 18 November 2026

AppCar Race Offline
DeveloperSamir Hayan

This policy explains what happens with your data when you play Car Race Offline (the "game"), a free car racing game for Android published on Google Play by Samir Hayan (the "developer", "we"). In short: we do not collect, sell or share any personal data. The game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Data we collect

None. Samir Hayan does not run any server that receives data from the game, and the game does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, player name, story progress and high scores on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

3. Offline play

Car Race Offline is a single-player game against computer-controlled racers. It has no online multiplayer, no online accounts and no chat, and the game itself does not send any data over the Internet.

4. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Extra tracks may be downloaded through Google Play when you ask for them. Google may process data such as your device information and IP address for these downloads and for app updates, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
